Candle Call

Will the current 5-minute and 15-minute candle close above its open? A model call from live Binance candles for BTC, ETH and BNB, next to the live odds on Binance Prediction's 5-minute and 15-minute Up or Down rounds.

Connecting to Binance…

How the call is made. For each timeframe the page loads the last 1,000 Binance spot candles and trains a small logistic model on the 500 candles before now. Inputs come only from candles that have already closed: the last candle's body and where it closed in its range, 3- and 12-candle momentum, RSI(14), the EMA 9/21 gap, volume against its 20-candle average and the share of taker buying. The output is the chance the current candle closes above its open. Calls between 47% and 53% show as "No edge".

Two calls per timeframe. This round is live: it starts from the model's call at the open and moves with the price — how far it is above or below the open, against the usual size of a move for the time left. Near the close it mostly follows the price. Next round is the model's call for the candle after this one, worked out as if the running candle closed now; it settles when this round closes and becomes the next round's opening call.

Hit rate is a walk-forward test on the last 200 closed candles: each one was called by a model trained only on candles before it. Compare it with the up-candle share; short-timeframe direction is close to a coin flip, and a hit rate a few points either side of 50% is normal. The call log is kept in this browser only.

Binance Prediction market shows the crowd's odds from the "BTC / ETH / BNB Up or Down 5m" and "… 15m" markets on Binance Prediction. Each round settles against its own reference price (the "Price to Beat" on the market card), which can differ slightly from the Binance spot candle's open, so a very close candle can settle differently. Market odds react to the price as the candle runs; the model's call uses only candles that had already closed when this one opened.

Data: Binance public market data (data-api.binance.vision), spot USDT pairs, and the public Binance Prediction page. Your browser reads them straight from Binance; nothing is stored or sent anywhere else. Not financial advice.
